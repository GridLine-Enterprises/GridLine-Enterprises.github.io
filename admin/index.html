1
Download gmaps-gridline.zip and extract it so you have the gmaps-gridline folder with manifest.json inside.
2
Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and choose that extracted folder.
3
Open the extension, click the gear icon, and paste this spreadsheet ID: 1MJ5sJqP-t0bWz93O_XYlCjoA7nU__mJ1T_PZT1Pr_K4.
4
Save the spreadsheet ID in settings. When prompted, sign in with the Google account that can edit the sheet.
5
